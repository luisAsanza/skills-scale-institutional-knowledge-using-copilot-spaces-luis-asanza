# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers research user needs, design intuitive interfaces, and validate usability throughout the product lifecycle. They ensure products are accessible, delightful, and aligned with customer expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and synthesize insights
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Collaborate with Product Managers on feature requirements
- Partner with Developers to ensure design feasibility and implementation quality
- Conduct usability testing and iterate based on feedback
- Maintain design system consistency and accessibility standards

### Goals
- Deliver user-centered designs that solve real customer problems
- Reduce friction and improve user satisfaction metrics
- Ensure accessibility compliance (WCAG standards)
- Maintain design system coherence across products

### Typical Communication
- Weekly design reviews with Product Managers and stakeholders
- Design critique sessions with other designers
- Handoff meetings with Developers to review specs and components
- User research readouts and usability test results
- Design documentation in Figma or similar tools

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on problem definition, success metrics, and feature prioritization
- **Developers**: Provide design specifications, review implementations, troubleshoot UI issues
- **Customer Support/Advocacy Lead**: Gather user feedback and pain points to inform design decisions
- **Project Managers**: Participate in sprint planning, report design progress and blockers

---

## Data Analyst / Data Scientist

### Role Summary
Data Analysts and Data Scientists turn data into actionable insights that drive product decisions. They build metrics, conduct experiments, and provide evidence-based recommendations to inform strategy and measure success.

### Responsibilities
- Define and track key product and business metrics
- Design and analyze A/B tests and experiments
- Build dashboards and data visualizations for stakeholders
- Conduct exploratory analysis to identify trends and opportunities
- Collaborate with Product Managers on success metrics and KPIs
- Work with DevOps Engineers to ensure data pipeline reliability

### Goals
- Enable data-informed decision-making across the organization
- Measure product impact and user behavior accurately
- Identify opportunities for optimization and growth
- Maintain data quality and governance standards

### Typical Communication
- Weekly metrics reviews with Product Managers and leadership
- Experiment design and results presentations
- Data documentation and methodology guides
- Dashboard reviews and metric definitions
- SQL queries and analysis notebooks

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Define success metrics, analyze feature impact, inform roadmap decisions
- **Developers**: Implement tracking and instrumentation, troubleshoot data collection issues
- **DevOps Engineers**: Collaborate on data pipeline architecture and monitoring
- **Project Managers**: Provide progress metrics and impact analysis for projects
- **UX Designers**: Share user behavior insights to inform design decisions

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers build and maintain the infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and operational tooling that enable reliable, scalable, and secure software delivery. They bridge development and operations, promoting automation and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation
- Manage infrastructure as code (IaC) and cloud resources
- Monitor system health, performance, and security
- Implement and maintain observability tools (logging, metrics, tracing)
- Respond to incidents and conduct post-incident reviews
- Collaborate with Developers on deployment strategies and reliability

### Goals
- Achieve reliable, automated, and fast deployments
- Minimize downtime and mean time to recovery (MTTR)
- Maintain secure and compliant infrastructure
- Enable developer productivity through self-service tooling

### Typical Communication
- Daily operations standups and on-call handoffs
- Incident response and post-mortem documentation
- Infrastructure change reviews and deployment notifications
- Capacity planning and performance reports
- Runbooks and operational documentation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on CI/CD pipeline design, troubleshoot deployment issues, implement observability
- **Project Managers**: Report on deployment readiness, infrastructure risks, and operational blockers
- **Data Analysts/Data Scientists**: Ensure data pipeline reliability and monitoring
- **Product Managers**: Communicate infrastructure constraints and enable feature rollout strategies
- **Customer Support/Advocacy Lead**: Provide incident status and resolution updates

---

## Customer Support / Advocacy Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Support/Advocacy Leads serve as the voice of the customer within the organization. They handle escalations, gather feedback, advocate for user needs, and ensure customers receive timely, effective support throughout their journey.

### Responsibilities
- Triage and resolve customer issues and escalations
- Gather and synthesize customer feedback for Product and Engineering teams
- Create and maintain customer-facing documentation and knowledge base
- Track support metrics and identify recurring issues
- Advocate for customer needs in product planning and prioritization
- Train and support customer success teams

### Goals
- Deliver excellent customer experience and satisfaction (CSAT/NPS)
- Reduce time to resolution for customer issues
- Identify and escalate product gaps and pain points
- Enable customer self-service through documentation and resources

### Typical Communication
- Daily triage and ticket reviews
- Weekly feedback synthesis and trend analysis
- Customer escalation reports to Product and Engineering
- Knowledge base articles and support guides
- Customer advisory board meetings and feedback sessions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Provide customer feedback, advocate for user needs, inform roadmap priorities
- **Developers**: Report bugs and issues, collaborate on troubleshooting and fixes
- **UX Designers**: Share user pain points and usability feedback to inform design improvements
- **Project Managers**: Report customer-impacting issues during releases, coordinate rollout communications
- **DevOps Engineers**: Escalate incidents, receive status updates, coordinate customer communications
