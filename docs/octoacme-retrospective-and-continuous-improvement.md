# OctoAcme — Retrospective & Continuous Improvement

## Purpose
Capture learnings and convert them into actionable improvements.

## When
After each sprint, release, or important milestone. Also after incidents.

## Structure
- What went well
- What could be improved
- Action items (owner, due date)
- Follow-up on previous action items

## Running a Retrospective
- Timebox: 45–75 minutes depending on team size
- Use an anonymous idea board if needed to encourage candor
- Prioritize 2–3 top action items to avoid overload
- Ensure all relevant roles participate to get diverse perspectives

## Role Participation in Retrospectives

All team members should actively participate in retrospectives to ensure comprehensive feedback and continuous improvement:

**Product Manager**
- Reflect on prioritization decisions and backlog health
- Share customer feedback and product insights
- Identify opportunities for better alignment with business goals

**Project Manager**
- Facilitate the retrospective meeting
- Capture action items and ensure follow-through
- Reflect on communication, coordination, and process effectiveness
- Document retrospective notes in project repository

**Developers**
- Share technical learnings and challenges
- Suggest process improvements for development workflow
- Reflect on code quality, testing, and collaboration

**UX Designer**
- Reflect on design process and handoffs
- Share usability insights and user feedback
- Suggest improvements for design-development collaboration

**Data Analyst / Data Scientist**
- Share insights from metrics and experiments
- Reflect on data quality and instrumentation effectiveness
- Suggest improvements for measurement and reporting

**DevOps Engineer**
- Reflect on deployment process and infrastructure reliability
- Share operational insights and incident learnings
- Suggest improvements for automation and monitoring

**Customer Support / Advocacy Lead**
- Share customer feedback and pain points
- Reflect on support handoffs and documentation quality
- Suggest improvements for customer communication

## Tracking Improvements
- Add action items to the project backlog or issues with clear owners and timelines
- Review outstanding actions in the weekly PM sync
