# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome! This README serves as the single entry point for all OctoAcme project management process documentation. Whether you're a new team member getting oriented or an experienced contributor looking for a specific process guide, this index will help you navigate our standardized practices and frameworks.

## Overview of OctoAcme Project Management Processes

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, outcome-driven approach to project management that emphasizes iterative delivery, quality, and continuous improvement. Our methodology is built on clear lifecycle phases that guide every project from initiation through retrospective: Initiation (problem definition and stakeholder alignment), Planning (scope, resources, and milestones), Execution (build, test, and iterate), Release (deployment and verification), and Retrospective (capturing learnings and improvements). This structured approach ensures repeatable, reliable execution while maintaining flexibility to adapt based on feedback and changing requirements.

Our project management framework relies on well-defined personas and roles to ensure clear ownership and accountability. Each project has a named Project Manager who coordinates delivery, schedules, risk management, and communications, working closely with a Product Manager who defines outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, and measures success. Developers implement features and collaborate on design and testability, while QA/Testing validates quality and acceptance criteria. Stakeholders provide critical inputs and approvals throughout the lifecycle. This explicit role definition eliminates confusion and enables effective collaboration across cross-functional teams.

Communication and transparency are foundational to our success. We maintain a regular cadence of touchpoints including weekly syncs between PM and Product Manager, twice-weekly standups for delivery teams, and monthly stakeholder updates, with ad-hoc escalations as needed. Quality assurance is embedded throughout the process rather than treated as a final gate—teams use templates and checklists to ensure consistency, maintain comprehensive acceptance criteria and Definition of Done, and track risks proactively in a shared risk register. Our retrospectives close every project cycle by capturing lessons learned and converting them into actionable improvements, creating a culture of continuous learning and adaptation.

Strong emphasis on data-informed decisions, psychological safety, and maintaining key artifacts (Project Charter, Roadmap, Sprint Backlog, Risk Register, and Retrospective notes) ensures that teams have the information and support they need to deliver customer value effectively. By combining structured processes with collaborative practices and a commitment to quality, OctoAcme empowers teams to ship reliable software that meets user needs while continuously improving how we work.

## How to Use These Docs

Keep your project artifacts updated in the `docs/` folder to maintain a single source of truth for project management processes. When working on specific projects, add process-specific documentation into the `.copilot/` directory if you want GitHub Copilot Spaces to use them as context for AI-assisted development and support. This approach ensures that both human team members and AI assistants have access to the right information at the right time.
